V080 JEWELS BASETYPE AUDIT
===========================
Target: NeverSink 0-SOFT 0.10.4

Purpose
- Add JEWELS as the next top menu after WAYSTONES and TABLETS.
- List what the loot filter can actually distinguish at drop time, not individual unique jewel names.

NeverSink unique jewel BaseTypes ([[2902]])
T1:
- Emerald
- Ruby
- Sapphire
- Time-Lost Diamond
T2:
- Diamond
- Timeless Jewel
=> Unique jewel settings: 6 BaseTypes total.

Important consolidation
- Multiple named unique jewels that share BaseType Sapphire (e.g. the three Voice variants shown in PoE2DB) are ONE filterable drop type: Sapphire.
- Therefore UI has exactly one Unique Sapphire row.

NeverSink non-unique Jewels ([[2000]])
- Time-Lost Emerald / Time-Lost Ruby / Time-Lost Sapphire: all Normal/Magic/Rare, special Time-Lost style.
- Emerald / Ruby / Sapphire: Rare style when Rare.
- Emerald / Ruby / Sapphire: Normal/Magic style when Normal or Magic.
