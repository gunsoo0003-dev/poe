Root cause:
V210 deleted the official export's trailing '(rune)' marker before sending the item to PoB,
thereby causing PoB to treat those lines as ordinary non-rune modifiers.
It also treated 'Socketed item effects require PoB verification' as an unconditional warning.

V211:
- Preserve source rune/enchant modifiers as native {rune} and {enchant} lines.
- Regroup into the native PoB Implicits section with a corrected count.
- Preserve Bonded: condition prefix; PoB itself governs CanUseBondedModifiers.
- Pass socket counts and unambiguous augment identities (exclude granted skills).
- Audit the actual Lua Item.runeModLines after equip, including count and values.
- Warn only for mismatches, unparsed rune lines, unknown Rune identities or an unsupported Bonded effect when active.
- Do not warn merely because sockets are present.
- Do not change PoB's Lua calculation algorithms.
